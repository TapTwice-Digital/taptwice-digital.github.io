Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

Overview

Arvio is a personal budgeting app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data Storage

All of your financial data — including transactions, budgets, categories, and settings — is stored locally on your device.

If you are signed into iCloud, your data may also sync to your private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit framework. That data is only accessible to you and anyone you explicitly choose to share a ledger with.

We do not have access to your private iCloud data. Apple manages the storage, syncing, and encryption of that information.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information for our own servers.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your financial data to third parties.
  • We do not operate servers that store your budgeting data.

Camera & Photo Library

Arvio may request access to your camera or photo library so you can attach receipt photos to transactions. These images are stored on your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud account. They are not uploaded to external Arvio-operated servers.

Third-Party Services

Arvio uses logo.dev to display company logos for recurring transactions. When a logo is requested, the company domain name may be sent to the logo.dev API. No personal or financial transaction data is included in those requests.

Logos may be cached locally on your device to improve performance.

iCloud Sharing

Arvio supports shared ledgers using Apple’s iCloud sharing features. If you share a ledger, the people you invite can view and edit the transactions, budgets, and related data contained in that shared ledger. Sharing is managed through Apple’s CloudKit infrastructure.

Children’s Privacy

Arvio does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the support page.
